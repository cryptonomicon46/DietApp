Privacy Policy

OurPawmily Families (beta) · Version 2026-10 · Last updated October 30, 2026

The short version. Your family is private: only the people you invite can see it. Your Diet and your Fitness are each visible only to the people you choose. We don't show ads, don't sell or share your data with advertisers, and don't track you across other apps or websites. You can download or delete everything from the app at any time.

What we collect

  • From Google when you sign in: your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. Only an email address Google has verified can sign in. We never see your Google password.
  • What you add:
    • your family's name
    • the names and relationships of the people you add, and the email addresses you send invites to
    • your pets' names, breeds, birthdays and care records (feedings, walks, treats, medications, vaccines, vet details)
    • threads, replies, chat messages, photos, albums and PDFs, and who uploaded each photo or PDF
    • 🙋 requests for a hand (what you need, the kind, an optional day and time, who's helping) and the family's 🛒 grocery list (each item, who added or ticked it, and when)
    • posts, comments and likes from the old family feed (no longer shown in the app, but kept, not deleted)
    • if you bring them over from the current app: your diet & fitness records (weigh-ins, meals, training, workouts) and other tracker data
  • To keep your account secure: the type of device and browser you signed in from, and when (e.g. "iPhone · Safari"), so you can see your sign-ins and sign out everywhere.
  • If you turn on notifications: the address your phone's push service (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft) gives for this app on that device, your time zone (so reminders come at the right local time), which kinds of notifications you want, and your pets' vaccine due dates (pet and date only).
  • Safety: what you report (a thread, a reply or a chat message, with who reported it and why) and people you block. A chat report keeps only which message it was and the reason, never what the message says, which stays end-to-end encrypted.

We don't collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or analytics about how you use the app. Progress photos you take in the trackers stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

Who can see what

  • Your family: the people in your family see its members, pets, threads, albums, dog-care log and grocery list; the grown-ups see 🙋 requests (kids don't). The family's creator can limit what each person uses — Home, Calendar, Threads — and the server enforces it. The family's creator sees how many times something in the family was reported.
  • Diet and Fitness: visible only to you, plus anyone you choose — Diet and Fitness separately, person by person (💪 Health, or Me › 💪 Who can see my Health). Nobody else can turn it on for you, and connecting a fitness device never shares anything. Your phone keeps your tracker data as three locked parts: Diet, Fitness, and Us (your 2-2-2-2 and date ratings, which only your partner gets); people receive only the parts you share. Your full copy, with linked calendar addresses and device sign-ins, is only yours. Workouts you build or log in 🏋️ Fitness (Today's Workout, saved workouts and your history) are locked on your phone too and follow your Fitness sharing. While a workout is in progress, your phone also keeps a copy of it on the device — locked with the family key the same way in an encrypted family — so a reload or a lost connection doesn't lose a set; it's removed when you complete or remove the workout. A weight you save on 💪 Health goes into your Diet like any weigh-in. Plan progress (on track, an estimated goal date) is worked out on your phone and sent nowhere. 💬 Describe it sends what you typed only when you tap ✨ Read it with Claude (or reads it on your phone if you choose Read on this phone instead); then those words (and nothing else — no name, history or other family data) sent to Anthropic's API to be read. The connector passes them on without storing them and keeps only a note that a request was made (its kind, size and whether it worked, for 30 days — never the words). 📷 Photo works the same way: nothing is sent until you tap Read it; then only that one picture goes to Anthropic, redrawn on your phone first so it carries no location or camera details. The connector doesn't store it, it isn't added to your workouts or anything else, and no other AI feature ever sees it. Until your phone has saved the parts once after the 2.0 update, someone you had already shared your diet & fitness with keeps seeing your full copy, as before. If two phones shared trackers the older way (before accounts), a phone signed in to OurPawmily stops sending yours that way; the last copy sent before then stays on the connector (readable with that older setup, and by 🛟 Recover) until it's removed.
  • 👔 Look (your Closet): the pieces you add, their pictures, your Saved Looks, what you wore when, and what you've liked or said no to are stored for you alone. Nobody else in the family — not your partner, not the family's creator — and no connected family can read them; the connector refuses anyone else, whatever they ask for. In an encrypted family they're locked on your phone with the family key first. Every grown-up in a family holds that key, so it's the connector's checks — not separate keys — that keep your Closet from other family members. Background removal and recommendations are worked out on your phone: today's events and the weather are read there, only a short label like "Dinner at 5:30 PM" is shown, and nothing about your day or your clothes is sent anywhere to choose a Look. A small summary of today's Look (the label, how many Looks are ready, the name of the one you wore) is kept on your phone so 🏠 Home and 📅 Calendar can show it without loading anything. Fit Check shares one Look on purpose: a picture of it (drawn on your phone), its name and your optional message go to your partner, who answers Hit or Miss; nothing else from your Closet goes with it, and Fit Checks are removed after two weeks or when either of you leaves the family. Find pieces and ✨ Name it with Claude send only that one picture (redrawn on your phone, so no location or camera details) to Anthropic, only when you tap (Name it with Claude, once you've used it, names the next pieces you add, and says so); they're not kept by us, and Anthropic doesn't train on them and deletes them after a short safety check period. Deleting a piece removes it from your Closet and from suggestions; a Look you wore keeps showing it until that Look is deleted. Leaving the family or deleting your account deletes your Closet, its pictures and your Fit Checks.
  • Connected families (friends' families you invite) see only the threads (and older posts) you shared with them, and the names of your family's members. They can open a photo or PDF only through the shared thread or post it's on, never one that tags someone your family keeps private, and nothing once the families disconnect. They never see email addresses.
  • Photos and PDFs: only the person who uploaded a file, or the family's creator, can replace or delete it, and a file can only be attached by the person who uploaded it.
  • The old family feed: the app no longer shows the feed. Posts already made are kept on the server, not deleted, until you delete your account, leave the family, or the family is deleted; a read-only view of them is planned.
  • Children under 13 don't have accounts. Parents can add them as profiles they manage, holding only what the parent enters.

Where it's stored and who processes it

  • Cloudflare hosts the app's server and stores your family's data, including photos, PDFs and dog documents (Cloudflare R2), which in an end-to-end encrypted family are locked on your phone before they're uploaded.
  • Google handles sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app's files.
  • Notifications are delivered by your device's push service (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft). Each one is encrypted so only your device can read it, and it says who did what (e.g. "Pat left you a note"), never what an encrypted note, plan or event says.
  • The trackers fetch weather from Open-Meteo. Those requests carry no personal data, only fixed city locations.
  • 💡 Date ideas: once a week the connector looks up events (Ticketmaster, SeatGeek), movies and their ratings (TMDB, OMDb) and uses Google's Gemini with Google Search (or, as a backup, Claude from Anthropic with web search) to find showtimes and new places to eat. It only ever sends your area (a ZIP code's town) and the dates — never names, plans or what you picked. Your 💗 picks are sent as codes made with your family key, so the connector can tell when you've both picked the same idea without knowing what it was. Links to OpenTable, Ticketmaster or a theatre open their own sites.
  • 📷 Albums: album names, Google Photos links and photos copied into an album are stored like threads (end-to-end encrypted in an encrypted family) and seen only by your family. When you pick photos from Google Photos, your phone asks Google for permission to see just the photos you pick, downloads them from Google, and locks them before they're sent; the connector never talks to Google Photos.
  • 📰 News and 🐶 rescue news: hidden for now. While they're hidden the app doesn't load them and the connector doesn't fetch them. If they come back, the connector reads public news feeds and a dog rescue's public listings and has Google's Gemini (or Claude) sum them up; it's the same for everyone and nothing about you is sent.
  • 🧭 Plan an outing: when you ask for one, the connector sends Claude (Anthropic, with web search) the place you typed, the day, the choices you tapped (like "Thai" or "Caltrain") and your home town, plus the one-line taste summary if 💞 Use our taste is on. Outings never go to Google's free Gemini service. The plan comes back to your phone; if you send it to your partner it's stored like any other plan (end-to-end encrypted in an encrypted family). Saved outings stay on your phone; the finished plan is also kept on the connector for one day (so asking again is instant and a phone that closed the app can pick it up), then deleted.
  • 📞 Check-in reminders: for "call Mom every two weeks", run / ride / hot yoga reminders and the morning weigh-in reminder (off unless you turn it on in 💪 Health), the connector keeps how often and the date you last did each (so it can send the reminder), under your account only — for the weigh-in, just the date you last weighed in, never your weight, and the notification itself has no numbers in it. Your workouts themselves stay in your encrypted trackers; your phone works out the last date.
  • 🎬 Movies: if you switch movies on, the connector keeps that setting and the movies you asked to be alerted about (their TMDB numbers and titles) under your account, to send the alerts.
  • 📚 Read: the books you add are kept like your shows: each book's details (title, author, cover, series) are end-to-end encrypted in an encrypted family; the connector keeps who added it, who sees it (the two of you by default, or only you, or the family) and each person's status, format (📖 / 🎧), optional progress and ★ — not the titles. Only the people who can see a book see each other's status for it. When you search, the connector looks up only the words you typed on Open Library (a free, public book catalogue run by the Internet Archive) — never who you are or what's on anyone's list — and keeps the answer in Cloudflare's cache for a day. Covers are fetched through the connector too, so Open Library doesn't see which covers your household looks at. Nothing about your reading goes to an AI service.
  • 📺 Episode dates: to show when new episodes of your shows come out, your phone asks the connector for a show's dates by its TMDB number (looked up from the title once); the connector fetches them from TMDB and keeps nothing about who asked. Which TMDB show each of yours is stays on your phone.
  • 💬 Chats: messages between the grown-ups in a family (one to one, and the Grown-ups group), and their photos, screenshots and PDFs (with the PDFs' names), are end-to-end encrypted in an encrypted family. The connector keeps who wrote in which chat and when, reactions, who has read up to which message, and who has the app open (to show "online" and to skip notifications for a chat you're looking at). Children never have chats: only people with their own account who weren't added as a child and aren't under 18 by the birthday on their profile. Notifications say who wrote, never what. Unsending a message deletes its text and files; leaving a family deletes your one-to-one chats in it, and deleting your account also deletes your messages in the group.
  • 📖 Threads: titles, write-ups, replies and poll choices are end-to-end encrypted in an encrypted family (threads shared with friends' families get their own key, like posts). The connector keeps who wrote what and when, who it's for, the board, who in the family has opened it (shown to the family as "Seen by"), who last moved it to another board, the names of boards your family adds (not encrypted, like chore names), votes and ▲s. Screenshots and PDFs added to a thread, images added to a reply, their captions and the PDFs' names are encrypted the same way (the connector sees only each file's size). It also keeps which threads you ☆ saved (only you see that) and when a reply was edited. 🔎 Search happens on your phone: the threads are unlocked in memory only while you search; nothing is stored and the connector keeps no search index. On the 💡 App feedback board, 📮 Send to the app's developer sends only the summary and details you see (and can change) in the form, with your email, as a help ticket — nothing is sent unless you tap Send this. When you tag someone, the connector keeps who was tagged (so it can notify them); the notification says who tagged them, never what the thread says. ✨ Tidy it up sends only the text you tap it on to Claude (Anthropic), with tagged names replaced by placeholders, and keeps nothing. Drafts stay on your phone.
  • ⭐ Ratings: how a date or outing went is kept in your own trackers, end-to-end encrypted in an encrypted family (your partner sees it if you share your trackers). Your phone uses it to put what you like first. When you plan an outing, a one-line summary (like "they love sushi; avoid long waits") goes with the request only while 💞 Use our taste is on — you see the exact line first.
  • If you use 💬 Help, your questions (and the account details needed to answer them, like your families' member names) are sent to Anthropic's API so Claude can answer. If you report a problem, the report and that help chat are kept for the app's maintainer for up to 180 days.
  • If the app hits an error, the error message and where it happened (no account or personal data) are counted so we can fix it.
  • 📈 Ask about my plan (Meals, signed in): nothing is sent until you've seen "Sends your plan numbers and your question to Claude" and tapped OK. Then each question sends Anthropic your plan's numbers only — phase and week, your calorie and macro targets, a seven-day summary (days logged and on target, average calories and protein), your weight trend and planned rate, the goal date, how many days you trained — with your question and this chat's earlier questions and answers. Never your name, meals or food notes, workout notes, weights, anyone else's Health, household content, threads or pets; the connector rebuilds the numbers through that list before sending. Claude can explain and suggest but can't change anything; a suggested change opens your weekly check-in, where nothing changes unless you approve it. Nothing is kept by us beyond a note that a request was made (its kind and size, never the words); Anthropic doesn't train on it and deletes it after a short safety check period. The chat itself isn't saved.
  • 🗣️ Talk to Pawmily: when you open Talk it starts listening (unless you turn that off): your browser's speech recognition turns your voice into text — on an iPhone through Apple's speech service, as with Siri dictation, on Android through Google's — under those companies' own privacy terms; OurPawmily never receives audio, only the text. What you say or type is read on your phone first. Only a request your phone can't read is sent to Anthropic so Claude can read it, and then only the words — with people's names in your family swapped for roles like "your partner" — plus which tab you were on and today's date. Your watchlist, books, plans, food log and anything else stay on your phone: titles are matched there. Claude only answers with steps for you to approve; nothing changes until you tap, and what you said isn't kept by us (a record that a request happened, without what it said, is kept for 30 days). Anthropic doesn't use API data to train its models by default and deletes it after a short safety check period. "Recommended by" on a book records which family member recommended it, locked like the book. Questions about your day ("What's on today?", "When should I go for a run?") are answered only after you allow it once in Talk: then your phone sends Anthropic today's and tomorrow's calendar as this app shows it to you — times, titles, places and whose each item is (family events, linked calendars, the dogs' appointments and plans, including ones your partner or family added to the shared calendar), with family members' names swapped for roles — plus the free time your phone worked out, today's weather when the app has it, and your own WHOOP summary (recovery, sleep, strain) when WHOOP is connected. Notes, other lists and history aren't sent, and nothing is kept by us. A suggested time is only added if you tap it, and only to your own calendar.
  • 🍽 Logging food in 💪 Health: meals, drinks and estimates you log are saved into your Diet, locked like the rest of it, and shared only as your Diet is. 📷 Photo: nothing leaves your phone until you tap Estimate it; then only that one picture — redrawn on your phone first, so it carries no location or camera details — is sent to Anthropic so Claude can estimate it, with whether it's a label or a restaurant dish. The photo isn't kept: not by us, not in your log; only the numbers you choose to log are saved. 🎙️ Tell me: your phone reads everyday foods itself; only words it can't place are sent to Anthropic, and only the words — OurPawmily never receives audio (your phone's keyboard turns speech into text, under your phone's own settings). Nothing else from your food log, plan or weight goes with either. Anthropic doesn't use API data to train its models by default and deletes it after a short safety check period.
  • If you use "Ask Claude" in the trackers, what you type and the data needed to answer it is sent to Anthropic's API. Anthropic doesn't use API data to train its models by default.
  • ⌚ WHOOP (if you connect it in 💪 Health › Devices): you sign in on WHOOP's own page and allow OurPawmily to read your workouts, each day's calories burned, strain and steps, and your Recovery and Sleep — nothing else (not your profile or body measurements). The sign-in it gives back is kept only on the device you connected on; the connector passes requests to WHOOP without storing them or the sign-in. Workouts and calories that come in become part of your Fitness and are shared only as your Fitness is. Recovery and Sleep are private to you (with the day's strain and steps that come with them): they're kept in your own copy of your trackers (locked with your family's key, like the rest), in no part anyone in your family can be given, whatever your Fitness sharing, and never sent to Claude or anyone else. What OurPawmily works out from them on your phone — whether Recovery is lower or higher than your usual, how much less you slept than WHOOP says you needed, a lighter version of today's workout — isn't stored; nothing about your workout changes unless you tap Apply. Disconnecting asks WHOOP to remove OurPawmily's access and deletes the sign-in from your device; workouts already brought in stay until you delete them. Garmin isn't connected to OurPawmily in any way.
  • How AI requests are handled: every request to an AI service goes through one checkpoint on the connector that allows only the fields that feature needs (anything else is dropped), sends personal content only to Anthropic (never to a free service that may review or train on it), limits how many requests a day each person can make, and keeps a 30-day note of each request's purpose, field names, sizes and whether it worked — never what was sent. Your family key never leaves your devices. Anthropic keeps API requests only for a limited time for safety checks and doesn't train on them.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Families are end-to-end encrypted: threads, chat messages, posts, photos, comments, notes, plans, requests, grocery items, diet & fitness data, your pets' details and appointment notes are locked on your family's devices with a key the server never sees, so neither we nor our providers can read them. The server can read what it needs to work: your account (name, email), the names of family members and pets, who's in which family, the chores log, games, dates of appointments, and a request's kind, day, time and who's helping. Your recovery backup is locked with a phrase only you have; if you lose every device and the phrase, your encrypted data can't be recovered by anyone.

How long we keep it

  • Your data: kept until you delete it or your account.
  • Sign-ins: expire after 30 days.
  • Invites: expire after 14 days or when used.
  • Problem reports: 180 days. Error counts: 30 days.
  • When you delete your account: your account, sign-ins, diet & fitness data, threads and replies you wrote (with their screenshots and PDFs), your requests for a hand, posts, comments and likes are deleted immediately (grocery items you added stay on the family's shared list until someone removes them) (see Deleting your account). Copies may linger in our providers' systems only for as long as their own retention allows.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Me › Account & security › Download my data (a JSON file).
  • Correct it: edit your name, pets and threads in the app.
  • Delete it: Me › Account & security › Delete my account, or leave or delete a family.
  • Sign out everywhere: Me › Account & security.
  • Contact us: use the support email shown in the app (Me › Account & security) or on the app's store listing.

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, the CCPA, or Washington's My Health My Data Act), you may have further rights to access, correct, delete or move your data. Write to us to use them; we'll reply within 30 days.

Health information

Diet & fitness data you add is used only to show it to you and the people you share it with. We never sell it or use it for advertising. You can delete it at any time. OurPawmily isn't a medical service, and nothing in it is medical or veterinary advice.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will ask you to review and accept the new version before you continue.
