Deleting your OurPawmily account

OurPawmily Families (beta)

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open OurPawmily and sign in.
  2. Go to Me › Account & security › Delete my account.
  3. Optionally tap Download your data first, then type DELETE to confirm.

Without the app

Email the support address shown on the app's store listing, from the Google address you sign in with, asking us to delete your account. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What's deleted

  • Your account (name, email, picture, Google account ID) and every sign-in on every device.
  • Your diet & fitness data and other tracker data, in every family.
  • Your posts and photos, comments, and likes.
  • Your place in each family.

What happens to families you created

  • A family you created passes to another grown-up who has joined it, so the rest of the family keeps its pets, posts and care log.
  • If nobody else has joined, the family is deleted with everything in it.
  • To delete a family for everyone first, use Me › Family settings › Delete.

What's kept

Nothing that identifies you. A shared dog-care entry stays in the family's log ("Walk · 8:05 AM") because it's the family's record, but your account is gone. Copies may persist in our hosting provider's systems only as long as their own retention allows.
