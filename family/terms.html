The beta

OurPawmily Families is a free beta. Features may change or stop working, and things may break. Keep the backup the app lets you download if your data matters to you.

Who can use it

You must be 13 or older to have an account. Parents can add younger children to their family as profiles they manage. You're responsible for the people you invite and for keeping your Google account secure; turning on Google's 2-Step Verification is strongly recommended.

Your content

You own what you post. You let us store it and show it to the people you share it with, only to run the app. Delete it at any time.

Be kind: what's not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, hate, or bullying.
  • Sexual content, and anything that exploits or endangers children. This is reported to the authorities.
  • Sharing someone else's private information or photos without their permission.
  • Illegal content or activity, spam, or trying to break into accounts or the service.

Anyone can report a post or block its author from the post's ⋯ menu. Reported posts are hidden for the reporter and flagged to the family's creator, who can remove them. We act on reports sent to the support email, generally within 24 hours, and can remove content or accounts that break these rules.

Not professional advice

Diet, fitness, weather, and pet-care information in the app is for everyday organizing, not medical or veterinary advice. Ask a professional about your health or your pet's.

Ending

You can stop using the app and delete your account at any time. We may suspend accounts that break these terms.
